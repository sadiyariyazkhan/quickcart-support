# QuickCart Customer Policy Handbook
*Version 3.2 — fictional company, created for a take-home assignment*

QuickCart is an online store selling electronics, home appliances, fashion and groceries across 15 Indian cities. This handbook is the single source of truth for customer-support answers.

---

## 1. Delivery
**1.1 Delivery timelines.** Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): 2–4 business days. Other serviceable cities: 4–7 business days. Groceries in metro cities: same day if ordered before 11:00 AM, otherwise next day.

**1.2 Delivery charges.** Free delivery on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 carry a ₹40 delivery charge. Grocery orders below ₹199 carry a ₹25 charge.

**1.3 Delayed deliveries.** If an order is not delivered within 2 business days after the promised date, the customer may raise a "Delivery delay" complaint. QuickCart credits ₹50 QuickCart Wallet money for every delayed order once the delay is confirmed. Delays caused by natural calamities, strikes or government restrictions are not eligible for this credit.

**1.4 Failed delivery attempts.** The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After 3 failed attempts the order is returned to the warehouse and a full refund is issued to the original payment method within 7 business days. Cash-on-Delivery orders that fail 3 times are cancelled without charge.

**1.5 Serviceable cities.** Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kochi, Indore, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Nagpur.

---

## 2. Order Cancellation
**2.1 Before dispatch.** Any order can be cancelled free of charge before it is dispatched, from the app or through customer support.

**2.2 After dispatch.** Once dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled; the customer may refuse delivery or request a return after delivery (see Section 3).

**2.3 Grocery orders.** Grocery orders can be cancelled only within 15 minutes of placing the order.

**2.4 Refund on cancellation.** Prepaid cancellations are refunded within 5 business days. No refund is needed for Cash-on-Delivery cancellations.

---

## 3. Returns
**3.1 Return window.** Electronics and appliances: 7 days from delivery. Fashion: 15 days from delivery. Groceries: not returnable, except when damaged, expired or wrong (report within 48 hours).

**3.2 Return conditions.** Items must be unused, with original packaging, tags, accessories, manuals and invoice. Fashion items must not be washed or altered.

**3.3 Non-returnable items.** Innerwear, cosmetics once opened, software and gift cards, personalised/custom-made products, and items marked "Non-returnable" on the product page.

**3.4 Return pickup.** Pickup is scheduled within 2 business days of the return request. The courier performs a quick quality check at pickup; if the item fails the check, the pickup is cancelled and the customer is informed by SMS.

---

## 4. Refunds
**4.1 Refund timelines.** After the returned item passes the warehouse quality check: UPI and wallets — 1–2 business days; debit/credit cards — 5–7 business days; net banking — 3–5 business days; Cash-on-Delivery orders — refunded to the QuickCart Wallet instantly, or to a bank account within 5 business days if the customer provides bank details.

**4.2 Partial refunds.** If an item is returned with missing accessories or minor damage, QuickCart may issue a partial refund of up to 50% of the item price after informing the customer.

**4.3 Refund not received.** If a refund is not received within the timeline above, the customer may raise a "Refund" complaint with the order ID. Support must share the bank reference number (UTR/ARN) within 2 business days.

---

## 5. Damaged, Defective or Wrong Items
**5.1 Reporting.** Damaged, defective or wrong items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with at least one photo of the product and packaging.

**5.2 Resolution.** The customer may choose a replacement (subject to stock) or a full refund. Replacement items are shipped within 3 business days of pickup of the original item.

**5.3 Missing items.** If the package is delivered but an item is missing, the customer must report it within 48 hours. QuickCart investigates with the courier and resolves within 5 business days.

---

## 6. Warranty
**6.1 Manufacturer warranty.** Electronics and appliances carry the manufacturer's warranty stated on the product page. Warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centre; QuickCart support can share the service-centre contact.

**6.2 QuickCart Assured.** Products marked "QuickCart Assured" get an extra 6 months of warranty handled directly by QuickCart.

**6.3 Not covered.** Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs and normal wear and tear are not covered under warranty.

---

## 7. Payments
**7.1 Accepted methods.** UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, QuickCart Wallet, and Cash-on-Delivery (COD) for orders up to ₹20,000.

**7.2 Payment deducted but order not placed.** If money is deducted but no order is created, the amount is automatically refunded within 3 business days. If not, the customer may raise a "Payment" complaint.

**7.3 EMI.** No-cost EMI is available on orders above ₹3,000 with selected credit cards for 3 or 6 months.

**7.4 Never share OTP.** QuickCart staff will never ask for an OTP, card PIN, CVV or UPI PIN. Customers who receive such requests should report them immediately as suspected fraud.

---

## 8. Complaints and Escalation
**8.1 Raising a complaint.** Complaints can be raised through the app, the website, chat or the voice assistant. Each complaint gets a ticket number in the format `TKT-XXXXXX`.

**8.2 Response time.** First response within 24 hours. Resolution targets: Delivery delay — 3 business days; Damaged/Wrong item — 5 business days; Refund — 7 business days; Payment — 5 business days; Other — 7 business days.

**8.3 Escalation.** If a complaint is not resolved within the target time, the customer may escalate it once to a senior support manager, who must respond within 2 business days.

**8.4 Fraud and safety.** Suspected fraud (unauthorised payments, someone asking for OTP) and product safety issues (overheating, smoke, fire, electric shock) are escalated to a human agent immediately and are handled 24×7. Customers facing immediate danger should contact local emergency services first.

---

## 9. Privacy
**9.1 Data use.** Customer phone numbers and addresses are used only for order delivery and support.

**9.2 Identity check.** Before sharing order or complaint details, support verifies the customer by the registered mobile number linked to the order.

**9.3 Data deletion.** Customers may request account deletion through the app; data is deleted within 30 days except where retention is required by law (e.g., invoices).

---

## 10. QuickCart Wallet
**10.1 Wallet credits.** Credits from delays, COD refunds and promotions are added to the QuickCart Wallet.

**10.2 Validity.** Promotional credits expire in 90 days. Refund credits do not expire.

**10.3 Transfer.** Wallet balance cannot be transferred to another account or withdrawn as cash, except refund credits, which can be moved to a bank account on request.
